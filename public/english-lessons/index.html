English Lessons

Learn English properly.

English lessons with Eddie Barber, in small groups or one-to-one, for every level. Real fluency, clear pronunciation and quiet confidence — online anywhere in the world, or in person in London.

Group Lessons

Learn in good company.

Small English classes of no more than six students, taught by Eddie online. Real conversation, gently corrected, with plenty of speaking time for everyone — the most natural way to build fluency and confidence, and to enjoy a little of British culture and small talk along the way. Classes are scheduled weekly and welcome all levels from intermediate upward. Many students begin here, and some add private lessons alongside.

One-to-One English

The most personal way to study.

Private lessons designed entirely around you: your level, your goals, your pace. Everything a proper English education covers — grammar and vocabulary, reading and writing, listening and speaking, and above all the confidence to use the language naturally. Whether you want everyday fluency, better conversation, or a genuinely polished command of English, one-to-one lessons move at exactly your speed. All levels from intermediate upward are welcome, and every lesson is taught by Eddie himself.

English for Children

One great story at a time.

Lessons for children and teenagers, built around the kind of books that make them want to turn the next page. Eddie read Harry Potter and The Lord of the Rings aloud to his own three children many times over, and he does all the voices. Reading, speaking, vocabulary and pronunciation, with real confidence at the end of it. One-to-one or with a sibling or friend, online or in London. More on English for children →

Pronunciation & Received Pronunciation

Be understood the first time.

Accent softening and pronunciation coaching with a native speaker, so your spoken English is clear, confident and easy to follow — and, if you wish, closer to received pronunciation (RP), the accent Eddie grew up speaking at Repton. Lessons target the specific sounds your first language makes difficult, with recorded, measurable progress. More on pronunciation and RP →

Business English

English for the room you are actually in.

For professionals who already work in English but want it to work harder: meetings, presentations, negotiation, professional writing, and the unwritten rules of British business life. Lessons are built from your real material — the deck, the email thread, the interview — not a textbook. More on business English →

How lessons work

Structured, measured, and taught by one man.

Your first conversation with Eddie is relaxed and free of tests: he listens, establishes your level, and agrees a plan. From there, lessons are taken in blocks rather than single one-off hours, so there is continuity and real progress rather than a scattering of sessions. Because Eddie holds the CELTA — Cambridge's teaching certificate — lessons are properly structured and you always know what you are learning and why. Lessons run one-to-one, or in small groups of no more than six, online in any time zone or in person in London.

And the polish, if you would like it

An added extra, never the main course.

Some students want more than the language: the manners, the small talk, the confidence to feel at home in English social and professional life. Where it helps, Eddie folds a little of that in — but only if you want it. The lessons are, first and last, about learning English well. More on English with etiquette →
