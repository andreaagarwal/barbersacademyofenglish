Why the Academy

Why learn with the Academy

Anyone can sell you English lessons.

There are apps for five pounds an hour and language schools on every corner of London. If all you need is vocabulary, they will do.

The Academy teaches something they cannot.

English as it is actually lived at the top of British life: what to say, how to say it, and everything that surrounds the words. Manners, tone, timing, the difference between correct and right. Language schools teach English as a subject. Eddie teaches it as a way of carrying yourself, because it is the way he was brought up.

One teacher, not a factory.

Every lesson at the Academy is taught by Eddie himself. No rotating staff, no junior tutors, no script. This limits how many students the Academy can take, which is rather the point.

Taught, not just spoken.

Charm is not a qualification. Eddie holds the CELTA, Cambridge's teaching certificate, so lessons are structured and progress is measured. You will know what you are learning and why.

Discretion as standard.

Many of the Academy's students are private people preparing for public lives: executives, families, young people bound for British schools. Enquiries and lessons are handled personally and confidentially by Eddie alone.
