For Advisers

For education consultants and advisers

Discreet, reliable, and rather good with nervous families.

If you place students in British schools or advise international families, the Academy offers a service your clients frequently need and rarely find: preparation not just in English, but in Englishness. The Pre-Boarding Polish Programme readies a child for interview and for life at a British school: spoken confidence, manner, etiquette, and expectations, taught one-to-one by Eddie Barber, who was educated at Repton and holds the Cambridge CELTA teaching certificate.

Advisers receive a written report on completion, suitable for the family and the school file. Programmes can be delivered online in any time zone or in person in London, and are scheduled around assessment and interview dates.

The Academy works with a small number of advisory firms and accepts referrals on terms that respect your client relationship: your client remains yours, communication runs through you where you prefer it, and referral terms are agreed simply and honoured properly.

To discuss working together, write to eddie@barbersacademyofenglish.com marked "Advisers".
