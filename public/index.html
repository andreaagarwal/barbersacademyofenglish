About Your Tutor

Learn English properly, from someone who always has.

Eddie Barber was educated at Repton, one of England's oldest and finest public schools, where proper English — spoken, written, and lived — was simply the air one breathed. Six decades later, it still is. From Repton he went up to the London School of Economics, where he took his degree, and he holds a PGCE, the University of London's postgraduate teaching qualification.

CELTA-certified and endlessly patient, Eddie teaches English that works — clear, confident and correct, from everyday fluency to the precise, well-turned phrase. His students, professionals and families from around the world, come to learn the language; the charm they pick up along the way is complimentary.

Away from the lesson table, Eddie is a qualified personal trainer, and he brings a coach's patience and discipline to every lesson.

"Anyone can teach you English. I'd rather teach you to be at home in it."

How it works

Beginning is simple — and rather civilised.

1

Send an enquiry

Tell Eddie a little about yourself — your English today, and where you'd like it to be. He replies personally, usually within a day.

2

Meet for a chat

A relaxed introductory conversation, online or in London, to assess your level and agree a plan. No tests, no pressure — just a proper conversation.

3

Begin your lessons

Regular lessons at times that suit you, with clear progress and plenty of encouragement. Before long, you'll sound like you belong.

Questions, answered properly

The things prospective students most often ask.

What is Barber's Academy of English?
Barber's Academy of English is a private English tuition service based in London, England. It offers one-to-one British English lessons online worldwide, small group conversation classes, and in-person lessons in London. All lessons are taught by founder Eddie Barber, a CELTA-certified English teacher educated at Repton School.
Who is Eddie Barber?
Eddie Barber is the founder and principal tutor of Barber's Academy of English. He is a CELTA-certified English teacher, educated at Repton School, one of England's oldest public schools, holds a degree from the London School of Economics (LSE), and a PGCE teaching qualification from the University of London. He teaches British English, pronunciation, business English and British etiquette to adult professionals, families and international students from London and around the world.
How much do English lessons cost?
Fees depend on the lesson format — private one-to-one, small group, or in-person in London — and how often you wish to study. Send an enquiry through the website or email eddie@barbersacademyofenglish.com and Eddie will reply personally with a recommendation and fees, usually within a day.
