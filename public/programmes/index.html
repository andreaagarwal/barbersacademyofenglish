Programmes

Programmes

Three ways to study with Eddie. Each is personal, structured, and limited in number. Fees on enquiry; lessons are taken in programmes, not single hours.

The Pre-Boarding Polish Programme

For families preparing a child for a British boarding school.

Twenty hours with Eddie, online or in London, in the months before entry or interview. The programme covers spoken confidence and clarity, interview manner and conversation, table and social etiquette, and what daily life at a British school is actually like, taught by a man who lived it at Repton. It concludes with a written report for the family and, where relevant, their education adviser. Typically delivered over four to eight weeks.

The London Experience

English, lived rather than studied.

For students visiting London: an intensive private programme that pairs daily lessons with the city itself. Conversation over afternoon tea, the National Gallery, a proper lunch, the small rituals of English life, each one a lesson in disguise. Arranged by the day or the week, entirely around you.

The Residency

Eddie comes to you.

For clients who prefer to study at home, in the office, or while travelling: Eddie can be engaged to teach in person, anywhere in the world, for engagements from a few days to several weeks. The rarest and most personal way to study with the Academy. By arrangement only.

Regular online tuition, one-to-one and in small groups, continues alongside all programmes, with particular strengths in pronunciation and RP, business English, English with etiquette and preparation for a move to Britain. Begin with an enquiry; Eddie replies personally, usually within a day.
