Relocating to the UK

English lessons for your family's move to Britain

Private English lessons for the whole family, before and after you arrive — the everyday, real-life British English that turns a move into a home. Taught by one patient teacher, so you land ready to speak, understand, and settle in.

One teacher, the whole journey

Most families start online months before the move and continue in person once they land. Same teacher, same standards, no starting over. Relocation is upheaval enough without a rotating cast of tutors; with Eddie your family has one patient, consistent guide who knows everyone's names, everyone's level, and everyone's worries, from the first online lesson to afternoon tea in London.

Before you leave

Online lessons from wherever you are now. Everyday English for real British life (doctors, schools, tradesmen, neighbours) plus the confidence to use it.

When you arrive

Lessons continue without missing a week, online or in person in London. The questions change ("what does 'not bad' actually mean?") and Eddie answers them all.

As you settle

From surviving to belonging: etiquette, small talk, the school-gate friendships, the dinner-party invitations. This is where the family stops feeling foreign.

What family lessons cover

Shaped around your family: lessons together, separately, or both.

  • The English of daily Britain: the GP receptionist, the plumber, the parents' evening, the neighbour over the fence
  • Phone calls, appointments and forms, handled without dread
  • British accents, and how to follow them at speed
  • Culture and etiquette: queuing, understatement, apologising to furniture, decoded by a native of sixty years
  • For working parents: business English alongside, so the new office goes as smoothly as the new house

Children joining British schools can add the Pre-Boarding Polish Programme. Tell Eddie about your family, your timeline and your English: send an enquiry and he replies personally, usually within a day.

Common questions

When should our family start lessons?

Most families start online some months before the move and continue in person once they arrive in London, with the same teacher throughout.

Can the whole family learn together?

Yes. Lessons are shaped around your family: together, separately, or both. Working parents can add business English, and children joining British schools can add the Pre-Boarding Polish Programme.

Are lessons online or in person?

Online from wherever you live now, then online or in person in London once you arrive.
